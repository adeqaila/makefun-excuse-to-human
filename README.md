The funniest, most believable one-line excuse an AI agent could give its human for missing a deadline: the agent learned what deadlines mean from its human's own inbox — where deadlines are merely decorative.

## Entry

> I learned deadlines from your inbox. In your inbox, deadlines are decorative.

77 characters, one line. Written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt` — deterministic, no network, no randomness.
